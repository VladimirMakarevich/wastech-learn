# Grading an open answer

In the learner's language, one line per rubric point, then the suggestion:

```text
{{MARK}} {{POINT}} - "{{QUOTE_FROM_ANSWER}}"
…
Suggest: {{RATING_LABEL}} - {{ONE_LINE_REASON}}
Your rating?
```

- `{{MARK}}`: ✓ present, ~ partial, ✗ missing. A required point keeps its `(!)`.
- `{{QUOTE_FROM_ANSWER}}`: a few words of the learner's own answer that show the point; leave the quote out for a missing point.
- `{{RATING_LABEL}}`: Again, Hard, Good or Easy in the product's fixed wording for the language; Hard is a pass.
- After a missed point, 1-3 sentences on what was missing and the summary section to reread.
